Privacy

What this site collects

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no forms, no ads and no cookies.

Visit statistics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit, which country they are in, what kind of device they use and which site referred them. It does not use cookies and does not identify or follow individual visitors.

Fonts, scripts and hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and scripts and map data from jsDelivr and cdnjs. As with any web request, these providers receive your IP address in order to deliver the files.

If you contact me

If you email me, I use your details only to reply to you. I do not sell or share them.
